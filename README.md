# Tea-leaf-Disease
Tea cultivation faces significant challenges due to various diseases, including gray 
blight, blister blight, and red rust, all of which significantly impact both the yield 
and quality of the tea produced. Traditional detection methods for these diseases 
are often labor-intensive and subject to human error, making them inefficient and 
subjective. This paper delves into the application of deep learning algorithms, such 
as VGG16, MySequential, and Inception, to create automated tea leaf disease 
detection systems. Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs), particularly VGG16, 
offer robust feature extraction capabilities that help detect diseases with high 
accuracy. MySequential and Inception architectures further enhance model 
performance through deeper network layers and a more efficient utilization of 
computational resources.Transfer learning is a key technique used in this approach, 
allowing the adaptation of pre-trained models to tea leaf disease datasets, thereby 
addressing challenges posed by data scarcity. Additionally, augmentation 
techniques and advanced neural network architectures contribute to precise disease 
localization and segmentation within tea leaf images. The integration of deep 
learning algorithms into tea leaf disease detection systems promises to deliver 
timely insights for farmers, enabling them to adopt proactive disease management 
strategies and promote sustainable tea cultivation practices.Ongoing research in 
this field remains essential to overcome existing challenges and fully realize the 
potential of deep learning in revolutionizing tea leaf disease detection and 
enhancing agricultural resilience. Through continued innovation, deep learning can 
transform traditional agricultural practices, making them more efficient, accurate, 
and sustainable.
